Workshops

Convening conflict scholars across methods

M2C2 convenes an annual mini-conference. Events are structured to encourage engagement across methodological approaches, combining panels of research by senior scholars and feedback sessions for junior fellows.

Call for Proposals

The call for proposals for the next M2C2 workshop is not yet open. Please check back, or contact the organizers with any questions.

When a call is active, we invite junior scholars — those not yet in tenure-track positions, including advanced graduate students and postdoctoral researchers — to submit working papers on topics related to political violence and conflict. We welcome proposals that are explicitly multi-method, but we also encourage submissions that use a single method: the goal is to showcase a diversity of perspectives across the conference, rather than within individual papers.
